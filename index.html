      About the data. The corpus comes from schyuler/Chamorro-Corpus-Builder: North Star Sunday readings (2017–2025), the Chamorro addendum to the 2010 Guam and CNMI Military Relocation EIS, and the booklet Matto Tatte Ginen I Lugat I Gerra. It is Chamorro only, with no English pairs, so it can confirm spelling and usage but cannot translate by itself.

      Accuracy. Translations are written by Claude and can be wrong, especially for idioms and for words the corpus doesn't contain (shown with a dashed underline). Check anything important with a fluent speaker or the Chamoru.info dictionary. Each translation is billed to your own Anthropic API account, usually a few cents.
